It is Labor Day weekend. Despite being in full-on hermitess mode, I can feel the stirrings of the joy of autumn. Every year, I anticipate the end of the hellish weather. I don’t like running around in 95 degree 95% humidity conditions. And even though this September is forecast to be warmer than usual (yay ecocide-induced climate change! 🙃), the longer nights at least mean more comfortable mornings. At any rate, I love Labor Day weekend because it means we are that much closer to cozy season & spooky season. 

I didn’t have a summer bucket list other than trying to save money for my group trip to South Lake Tahoe in August. It was a wonderful time with friends I’ve known for 20 years. I visited my sister & nephews in June. Tried to introduce my son to fireworks in July. (That did not go well LOL.) Got my mom acclimated to her new apartment. Recommenced therapy. Learned how to tighten the door handles around the house. 

I think I should make a list of goals for this last portion of the year, though. I need something to work toward. Something to be excited about. Don’t get me wrong, seeing my son grow and learn is an indescribable joy. But that’s him. That ain’t really me. 

Job-hunting is exorbitantly depressing. Frankly, a lot of my melancholy can be traced to that aspect of my life. I’m happy to be employed, but I really screwed up career-wise. Maybe that’s too harsh. I’m glad I switched careers after having my baby. It just happened at a time when things in the market were going haywire. All is not lost. I just have to work on my pivot. All that to say, I will **not** be adding “Get a new job” to my autumn bucket list because I don’t even want to see anything about a damned job on a list that is supposed to be positive. 

So to celebrate summer’s end, I’ll come up with a few things to do during my favorite season. These will not have anything to do with little man. Trust me, there’s already a massive list of motherhood goals. 

To start:

- Bake a loaf of bread with yeast. (I don’t know why yeast has always intimidated me. As much as I enjoy baking, I’ve avoided yeast-based recipes.) 
- Get some new waist beads. 
- Get back to doing 20 **real** push-ups. 
- Be more engaged with my mom group & community work. 
- Put more adult social events on my calendar and attend **at least one** per month. 
- Read at least 5 books on my [Spooky Season 2026 reading list](https://fable.co/list/da0f9322-e8cc-4d44-a10d-a8bbe64681e9/share). 
- Plan my Portugal trip. 
- Write more. Here. In my physical journals. On AO3. Doesn’t matter. Just write more. 

I can definitely do those things between now and early December. 

Well, it’s 5:30 am, & I have a day of hosting in front of me. We’ll see how well I can socialize off 4 hours of sleep. 

Peace. ✌🏾
